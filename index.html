Islamabad, The Beautiful

Islamabad is the federal capital of Pakistan, serving as the nation's political and administrative hub. Built as a planned city in the 1960s and officially inaugurated on 14 August 1967, it replaced Karachi as the capital to provide a more centrally located and secure seat of government.
